What is CyVerse?

CyVerse (formerly iPlant Collaborative) is a cyberinfrastructure platform designed to handle huge datasets and complex analyses for life sciences research. Think of it as:

  • A data management system - Store, organize, and share large datasets
  • A computational platform - Run analyses without owning expensive hardware
  • A collaborative workspace - Share data and analyses with colleagues
  • An integrated ecosystem - Multiple tools working together seamlessly
  • A learning community - Extensive tutorials, webinars, and support

CyVerse is particularly powerful for genomics, bioinformatics, and image analysis, providing researchers with the tools needed for data-intensive science.

Why Use CyVerse?

TipBenefits of Using CyVerse
  • Free storage - 100 GB base allocation, with options to request more
  • Powerful computing - Access to high-performance computing resources
  • Scalability - Handle datasets from gigabytes to terabytes
  • Reproducibility - Document and share complete workflows
  • No software installation - Cloud-based tools ready to use
  • Data sharing - Easy collaboration and data publication
  • FAIR principles - Supports Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable data
  • Community support - Active user community and extensive documentation

How to Create a CyVerse Account

Step 1: Go to CyVerse Website

Navigate to https://cyverse.org in your web browser.

Step 2: Start Registration

  1. Click “Get Started” or “Sign Up” button
  2. You’ll be directed to the CyVerse User Portal
  3. Click “Sign Up” or “Register”

Step 3: Fill Out Registration Form

Provide the following information:

  1. Username - Choose a unique username (lowercase, no spaces)
  2. Password - Create a strong password
  3. Email address - Your valid email (institutional preferred)
  4. First and Last Name - Your full name
  5. Institution - Your university, company, or organization
  6. Department - Your specific department or unit
  7. Position - Student, Postdoc, Faculty, etc.
  8. Country - Your location
WarningImportant
  • Use your institutional email if possible
  • Remember your username - you’ll use it to log in to all CyVerse services
  • Choose a username you’ll be comfortable sharing (it may be visible to collaborators)

Step 4: Agree to Terms

  1. Read the Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy
  2. Check the box to agree
  3. Complete any CAPTCHA verification
  4. Click “Register” or “Submit”

Step 5: Verify Your Email

  1. Check your email for a verification message from CyVerse
  2. Click the verification link in the email
  3. This confirms your email address and activates your account
Note

The verification email may take a few minutes to arrive. Check your spam folder if you don’t see it.

Step 6: Complete Your Profile

After email verification:

  1. Log in to the CyVerse User Portal
  2. Complete additional profile information (optional but recommended):
    • Research interests
    • ORCID iD (links your research identity)
    • Profile picture
    • Bio or description

Getting Started with the Discovery Environment

The Discovery Environment (DE) is CyVerse’s primary interface for data analysis. It’s a web-based platform where you can manage data, run analyses, and create workflows.

Step 1: Access the Discovery Environment

  1. Go to https://de.cyverse.org
  2. Click “Login”
  3. Enter your CyVerse username and password
  4. Click “Sign In”

Step 2: Take a Tour

When you first log in, you’ll see:

  • Top Menu Bar - Access different DE sections
  • Data - File and folder management
  • Apps - Available analysis tools
  • Analyses - Track your running and completed jobs
  • Teams - Collaborate with research groups
  • Collections - Curated datasets and apps
TipFirst-Time Users

The DE offers an interactive tour. Click the “?” icon or “Help” menu to start it!

Step 3: Navigate Your Data Store

  1. Click “Data” in the top menu
  2. You’ll see your personal folder structure:
    • /username - Your home directory (private by default)
    • /username/analyses - Output from your analyses
    • Community Data - Public datasets shared by others
    • Shared With Me - Data others have shared with you
